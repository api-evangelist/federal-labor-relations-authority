# Federal Labor Relations Authority (FLRA)

> The Federal Labor Relations Authority is an independent US federal agency that administers the labor-management relations program for the federal workforce under Title VII of the Civil Service Reform Act of 1978 (5 U.S.C. Chapter 71). It resolves unfair labor practice complaints, representation petitions, negotiability disputes, arbitration exceptions and bargaining impasses, and publishes its decisions.
>
> AGENT NOTE — THERE IS NO API. As of 2026-09-09 the FLRA operates no public API, no developer portal, no SDK and no machine-readable contract of any kind. Probes of /openapi.json, /swagger.json, /api, /api-docs, /developers, /developer, /jsonapi, /graphql and every named /.well-known/ path on www.flra.gov returned 404; api.flra.gov, data.flra.gov and developer.flra.gov do not resolve in DNS. The only machine-readable surface the agency publishes is the RSS feed set below. Everything else on the site is HTML rendered by Drupal.
